About Agrabad Degree College

Established in 1995 to foster academic rigor, ethical leadership, and high graduation standards in Chattogram, Bangladesh.

Institutional Profile

History & Heritage

Agrabad Degree College was founded in 1995 with the visionary goal of bringing accessible, disciplined, and world-class higher secondary and undergraduate education to students in the port city of Chattogram.

Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Chattogram for its Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) programs and the National University of Bangladesh for Degree Pass and Honours programs, the college has produced thousands of successful graduates who now serve in medicine, engineering, business management, civil service, and research worldwide.

Our Core Mission

Academic Excellence with Integrity

To cultivate critical analytical skills, scientific curiosity, moral responsibility, and lifelong learning attitudes in every student through dedicated mentorship.

National Human Resource Development

To equip the youth of Bangladesh with modern information and communication technology skills, professional knowledge, and patriotism.
